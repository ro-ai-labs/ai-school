{%- comment -%} Structured data (JSON-LD) for search engines and AI assistants. It says, on every page, what this site is, who writes it and which other projects are his, and then describes the page itself. The facts about the author come from _data/person.yml, the projects from _data/projects.yml and the FAQ from _data/mentorship.yml, the same files the visible pages are built from. Two kinds of "@id" are used: - The site, the school and the author each have one id and one address, the same on English and Romanian pages, so a reader that merges nodes by id never sees two answers. - Everything about a single page (the page, its breadcrumb, its article) has an id that starts with that page's own URL. Included by head.html, which sets page_title, share_title, page_description_plain, canonical_url, share_image, site_title and site_description. Every value goes through "jsonify", which quotes and escapes it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign me = site.data.person -%} {%- assign root = site.url | append: site.baseurl_root -%} {%- assign lang_root
